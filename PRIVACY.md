# Refundavio privacy policy

Effective 15 November 2026.

Refundavio helps you track purchases, returns, refunds, merchant follow-ups and warranties. You can use it without creating an account. The app stores the records and preferences you enter on your device. It contains no analytics or advertising SDK.

Optional Premium purchases and restores use your Amazon account. Refundavio sends the Amazon user ID and Premium receipt ID to its verification server, which checks them with Amazon to confirm ownership. The verifier processes those identifiers for the request and does not store them. Your Refundavio records are kept on your device.

When you attach a document, you choose it through your device’s document picker. Refundavio stores a reference to the document and keeps the file with its original provider. The provider and any app you use to open the document handle it under their own privacy policies.

Voice shortcuts are optional. If you tap **Speak**, the speech recognition service on your device processes the audio and returns a transcript to Refundavio. If you enable spoken summaries, the device’s text-to-speech engine receives the text it reads, which can include refund totals. Those services may use the internet and follow their own privacy policies. You can type or select a shortcut and keep spoken summaries off.

Reminder notifications use generic text. When you copy a draft, Refundavio puts the selected text on your device’s clipboard so you can paste it into an app of your choice.

If you choose to export a report, it can contain private purchase records, refund amounts and merchant contact notes. Refundavio asks you to confirm the export and choose a destination through your device’s file picker. The storage provider you choose controls the saved copy under its own privacy policy. Deleting records in Refundavio does not delete copies you exported.

The merchant directory stores the contact information and notes you enter on your device. Opening a saved merchant website hands the address to your device’s browser or another app that handles the link. Erasing all purchase data also removes saved merchant profiles.

Your purchase records stay on the device until you delete them. To remove those records and their refund and contact history, open **More → Erase all purchase data**. To remove preferences and all other app data, use your device’s app storage settings to clear Refundavio’s data, or uninstall it. Files you attached remain with their document provider.

For questions, contact the project through its GitHub issue tracker. Issues are public, so leave out receipts, order numbers, account details and other personal information. [Refundavio support](https://github.com/lanray07/Refundavio/issues)
